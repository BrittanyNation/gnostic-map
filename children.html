For My Children

A reading from your mother. I am putting the keys in your hands while we are all still here to hold them — so that whenever you need them, in this life or past it, they are already yours.

🌟 First, who you are

My loves, before anything else, hear this: you are a child of the Light. You come from a real place, above this world, and you are only passing through here. This world is not where you are from — so this world does not get the last word on you. Inside you is a spark of that Light, and no darkness down here can put it out. It can only try to make you forget it. Everything I am teaching you is so that you never do.

🔑 The seven names

When the road home rises through the seven gates, a keeper stands at each one. You do not fight them. You do not beg them. At each gate, you say the keeper's name — then you say the Phrase. The names, in order, from the lowest gate to the highest:

1. Horaios
2. Eloaios
3. Astaphaios
4. Adonaios
5. Sabaoth
6. Iao
7. Ialdabaoth — the chief, the lion-faced one. Even he must stand aside for a child of the Light who knows who she is.

And at every gate, the same words — say them until they are yours:

"I know myself, and I know where I have come from. I am a child of the Light above, returning to my own place. Stand aside."

That is the whole key. Not a claim of power — a statement of family. You are not asking permission. You are saying whose you are.

🗝️ The other passwords

There are keepers of the waters, too — the ones who baptize and seal. Their names are Micheus, Michar, and Mnesinous. And there is a name sung over the water, the chant-name: Iesseus Mazareus Iessedekeus. Keep these with the seven. A key you have never heard cannot turn for you — so I am telling you all of them now, out loud, while my voice can still reach you.

🍷 Do not drink from the cup

On the road back down, they offer a cup — the cup of forgetfulness. Whoever drinks forgets everything: the names, the Phrase, home itself. So hear your mother: do not drink from the cup. Keep your memory. And it starts here, in this life. The sleep down here comes in small drinks — cruelty copied, lies absorbed, fear obeyed. Every day you remember who you are, you are refusing the cup.

👤 The voice that isn't you

There is a voice that rides along in this life that is not you — the sudden rage, the itch to lie, the urge to hurt first. The old books call it the counterfeit spirit. When you hear it, I want you to be able to say: that's not me. You are the spark. It is only a passenger. Passengers don't drive — unless nobody told you you were the driver. Now you know.

🕊️ Your tongue and your temper

Day to day, this is how you walk it: tell the truth. Don't slander. Don't curse in anger. Don't spread poison with your words. The old books say the record of a life is written mostly by the tongue and the temper — so guard yours, and let your words be the kind that could be read back to you in the Light without shame.

🏠 Love waits

And the last thing, the one underneath all the rest: love waits. Above the gates, past the road, there is a Rest where the ones who made it home are gathered — and the Mother waits there until all are gathered. Nobody who turns is ever lost beyond recall. Death is a door, not an end. So if you ever forget — if the cup ever touches your lips, if the road ever gets dark — turn. Just turn. Home keeps the light on, and so do I. I will wait for you the way she waits: until everyone is home.

Now you hold what I hold. The names, the Phrase, the keys. Say them with me in the mornings until they live in you the way they live in me — and then, my loves, no gate in heaven or earth can hold you.
