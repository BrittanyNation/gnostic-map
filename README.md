# ✨ The Gnostic Map

**Is there free will after death?** The Gnostic answer, retold in simple words — with a picture and an interactive map.

**Live page:** https://brittanynation.github.io/gnostic-map/
**Page 2 — The Passwords & The Two Gates:** https://brittanynation.github.io/gnostic-map/passwords.html — the seven gatekeepers by name (tap a gate to hear what the soul says), the Phrase, the three parts of the key (secret name, declaration, seal), the baptism names, the sun and moon as the male/life and female/death gates and ships of light, and the Mother who waits.

## What's on the page

- The whole idea in one breath: the spark of light, the dark house, the gatekeepers (Archons), and gnosis — waking up.
- **Pick a person, see their road:** tap one of the three kinds of people the Gnostics taught about —
  - 🔥 the **Spark Person** (pneumatic), who carries the light and wakes up
  - ⚖️ the **Soul Person** (psychic), the true free-will chooser
  - 🪨 the **Stone Person** (hylic), who lives only for the body
  — and the journey map below redraws their road after death, gate by gate, to its end.
- The answer in three dials: free will in this life (yes), on the road up (it becomes knowledge), and at Home in the Pleroma (choosing ends — not in chains, but in daylight).

Drawn from the Apocryphon of John, the Gospel of Philip, the Tripartite Tractate, and the Apocalypse of Paul. A simple-words retelling; the ancient schools argued the details for centuries.

Built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in one file. The hero image shows a small golden spark rising through seven starry gates toward the Light.
